I'm building a South African fintech payment & savings platform for the 
EDHE Hackathon 2026 Market Connectivity Challenge.

PROJECT NAME: UKHONA PAY
(Meaning "There is" in Zulu - representing availability/existence of market connectivity)

CHALLENGE: Market Connectivity (100%)

VISION:
Connect informal vendors (spaza shops, taxi drivers, service providers) with 
corporate buyers through a unified digital payment ecosystem. Every transaction 
= payment for vendor + savings for corporate employee. Cashback withdrawn only 
as physical cash at ATMs (not bank transfers).

TECH STACK:
- Frontend: React.js with Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Java Spring Boot
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Integration: ABSA Bank ATM network (mocked for MVP)
- QR Code: For vendor payment scanning

TARGET USERS:
- Vendors (spaza shops, taxi drivers, service providers in informal economy)
- Corporate employees (reimburse vendors via app)
- ABSA Bank (provides ATM settlement infrastructure)

MARKET PROBLEM:
- 1.2M+ South African informal vendors geographically isolated (1-5km reach)
- Cannot reach institutional corporate buyers nationally
- Informal economy fragmented, no digital connectivity
- Cash-only transactions create security risk & no digital footprint
- No way for employees to reimburse vendors securely

SOLUTION:
- UKHONA PAY: Digital marketplace connecting vendors to corporate buyers
- Each transaction = money for vendor + savings for corporate employee
- Savings can ONLY be withdrawn as physical cash at ATMs
- QR code payment system for instant vendor payment
- No bank transfers - all withdrawals at ATM locations
- Cashback incentivizes platform use
- ABSA provides ATM settlement infrastructure
- Entire informal economy becomes connected & formalized

MARKET CONNECTIVITY - HOW IT WORKS:

BEFORE UKHONA PAY:
   Vendor → Serves only local community (1-5km radius)
   Employee → Carries cash to reimburse vendors (insecure)
   ABSA → Doesn't know informal vendors exist

AFTER UKHONA PAY:
   Vendor → Digital identity, visible to corporate buyers nationally
   Employee → Secure digital payment, auto-reimbursement
   ABSA → Sees vendor ecosystem, can offer lending products
   
RESULT: Fragmented informal economy becomes connected digital network
