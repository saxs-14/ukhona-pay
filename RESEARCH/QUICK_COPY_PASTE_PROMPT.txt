I'm building UKHONA PAY - a South African fintech payment & savings platform
for the EDHE Hackathon 2026 Market Connectivity Challenge.

PROJECT NAME: UKHONA PAY
(Meaning "There is" in Zulu - representing availability/existence of market connectivity)

CHALLENGE: Market Connectivity (100%)

VISION:
Connect informal vendors (spaza shops, taxi drivers, service providers) with 
corporate buyers through a unified digital payment ecosystem. Transactions = 
vendor payment + employee savings. Cashback withdrawn ONLY as physical cash 
at ATMs (not bank transfers).

TECH STACK:
- Frontend: React.js + Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Java Spring Boot
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Integration: ABSA ATM network (mocked)
- QR Code: For vendor payment scanning

TARGET:
- Vendors: spaza shops, taxi drivers, service providers
- Employees: corporate buyers who pay vendors
- ABSA: ATM settlement infrastructure

CORE FEATURES (MVP):

1. PAYMENT & WALLET
   - Digital wallet for all users
   - Send/receive money P2P
   - Transaction notifications
   - Transaction history

2. QR CODE PAYMENT
   - Vendors generate unique QR codes
   - Employees scan to initiate payment
   - Enter amount and confirm
   - Instant payment to vendor

3. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
   - Vendor profile (name, category, location)
   - Phone verification + OTP
   - Upload business photo
   - Generate QR code
   - View earnings & ratings

4. CORPORATE/EMPLOYEE
   - Search vendors by category/location
   - Scan QR code to pay
   - Quick pay buttons (R50, R100, R200)
   - Save favorite vendors
   - Ratings & reviews

5. SAVINGS & CASHBACK
   - Earn 2-5% cashback per transaction
   - Cashback auto to savings wallet
   - View total cashback earned
   - NO bank transfer option
   - ONLY withdrawal: Physical cash at ATM

6. ATM CASH WITHDRAWAL
   - Request cash withdrawal from app
   - Generate withdrawal PIN/OTP
   - Show nearby ATM locations
   - Go to ATM, enter PIN, withdraw
   - All withdrawals at ABSA ATM network (mocked)
   - Withdrawal confirmation in app

7. TRANSACTION TRACKING
   - Complete transaction history
   - Timestamp, amount, vendor, status
   - Filter by date/category/amount
   - Export transaction report
   - Real-time updates

8. DASHBOARDS
   Vendor: earnings, transaction count, ratings, withdrawal history, QR code
   Employee: spending, cashback earned, savings chart, category breakdown, 
             recent transactions, ATM withdrawal history
